# recallrival
A simple game to challenge your memory and outlast your rival.


## How to Play?

1. Eight dots are placed around a circle. This is your game area.
2. Choose your opponents: AI, a local friend, a remote friend, or a random player.
3. The first player taps any one of the eight dots. This starts the sequence.
4. The next player must tap the first player's dot, then tap one additional dot of their choice.
5. From then on, each player must tap the **entire sequence in the same order**, then add one new dot at the end.
6. The sequence grows by one dot after every turn, making it increasingly difficult to remember.
7. A player is eliminated if they tap the wrong dot.
8. The last player remaining wins the game.
